## About (Overview)

MH PharmaPack is a sourcing and supply partner for the pharmaceutical industry. We connect you with the right approved manufacturer and coordinate the entire engagement. We do not manufacture; we source, match, and manage, so a single point of contact carries your requirement from first enquiry to delivered goods.

What we source:
• API: active pharmaceutical ingredients across therapeutic categories.
• Finished formulations: finished dosage forms, from formulation development to supply.
• Packaging: primary and secondary pharmaceutical packaging, sourced to specification.

How we support every engagement: business development, supplier audit support, QA / QC and dossier coordination, documentation and paperwork, payment terms, and collection and logistics follow-up.

We source exclusively from WHO GMP and EU GMP approved plants across India, Latin America, and South Africa.

We coordinate and support; we do not assume the manufacturer's regulatory or quality responsibilities, and contracts of sale are directly between buyer and supplier.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Request a quote at mhpharmapack.com/contact
